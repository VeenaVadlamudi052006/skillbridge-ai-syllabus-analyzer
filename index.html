Research basis

D. Sunday, F. Jingo, and J. Kimbugwe (2026)

Study: “An AI-based framework for continuous alignment between higher education and industry skill needs,” Discover Education, Springer. DOI: 10.1007/s44217-026-02083-8.

Methodology: NLP, Sentence-BERT and RAG to extract skills from job postings and university syllabi, perform semantic alignment, and calculate skill gaps.

Outcome: Identifies curriculum–industry skill gaps and generates curriculum recommendations.

Dataset: Job postings, employment databases, programme documents and course syllabi.

Limitations: No expert validation or baseline comparison. The pilot corpus uses non-probability sampling, and continuous/streaming resynchronisation is not fully implemented.
